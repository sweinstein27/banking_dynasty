| 2026-11-05 | How do state bonds work? | `buy_bond` (one action) buys face value at the current market price, gated by the state's `min_reputation` and by free cash above the reserve. Coupons are paid quarterly on face value, including the quarter of purchase. Price is `1 - price_sensitivity * risk * (m - 1)` (floor 0.2): par when calm, lower when uneasy or panicking, recovering when calm returns. Holdings are marked to the new mood's price at the end of each turn, with a `revaluation` ledger entry, so the bonds account always equals face times price. Each quarter one default roll is made per state held (`p0 * m * risk`); a default writes off every holding of that state with no recovery, and the state sells no new bonds. States not held are not rolled, so holding nothing draws no extra randomness. No underwriting fee and no maturity for now. No voluntary sale (agreed default). |

| 2026-11-05 | How do bank runs, forced sales and bankruptcy work? | In a panic depositors withdraw 20% of deposits a quarter (`sentiment.withdrawal_share.panic`). Any shortfall, in any mood, is covered by selling active loans, oldest first, at a 30% discount (`forced_sale.loan_discount`); bonds and ventures join the sale list when they arrive. If everything is sold and depositors still cannot be paid, `house.status` becomes `bankrupt`: End Turn throws and every decision is refused. Withdrawals come after this quarter's interest and repayments, so money arriving that quarter can save the house. Sold loans have status `sold` and write a cash entry for the price and a loans entry for the full principal. |
