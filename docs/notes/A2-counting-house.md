| 2026-11-07 | Facilities did not pay: what should each do? | **Decided by Scott:** richer effects, then reception rooms that warm the houses rather than lift reputation. Measured first on main (775889d), each contender building one facility in its first year against building nothing, 300 seeds: nearly every facility lowered the median and raised bankruptcy (cautious 55,101 fl. and 3.3% with nothing; 42,153 to 49,626 and 4.3% to 7.3% with any facility). Medians of bimodal results (aggressive is 35% bankrupt) proved too noisy, so later checks were paired (the same seeds with and without, mean difference and its standard error) and then whole 1,000-seed runs. **Now (data/counting.json):** strongroom (3,000 fl., upkeep 10): panic withdrawals x0.5 (was 0.75), and in calm quarters deposits are drawn toward 1.5 times the level the house's reputation draws (in the counting step beside the tellers: the strongroom closes the usual catch-up share of the band above the reputation's level). Courier stable (1,500, upkeep 10): warnings at once, and bonds are bought 4% below the market, the gap booked as a `revaluation` gain (a hook in src/sim/bonds.ts: `courierBondEdge`). Reception rooms (2,000, upkeep 10): besides courting +10 and marriages at 10 less opinion, at each year end every trading house's baseline (where its opinion drifts back to) rises by 1, up to 25 (`warmHouses`; a direct opinion bump is supported but set to 0). Archive and warehouse unchanged. **Why reception rooms do not lift reputation:** at +2 reputation a year they stood in for educating the heir (T7 failed: cautious 55,014 against 72,357 without family). Pricing the rooms out of reach on the same seeds restored main exactly (51,986 and 47,452); the house without family suffers about 0.9 untrained successions a run (cautious 0.27), and the rooms repaired that reputation cheaply. A ceiling of 70 did not help. **Why the warmth is small:** at opinion +5 and baseline +3 a year up to 40, the rooms lifted cautious from 51,986 to 78,239 (better matches and dowries) and cautious dominated the investor (T2); at baseline +2 to 30 it was 75,923, at +2 to 25 69,620 (still edging the investor on all three measures). Baseline +1 to 25 leaves a clear margin. **Harness (src/harness/systems/counting.ts):** each strategy builds, before its other wants in the first quarter, what paid for it: cautious and cautious without family reception rooms; aggressive and bond-heavy a courier stable (bond-heavy builds it late otherwise, after it has bought its bonds, and gains nothing); the investor a strongroom (investor on 1,000 seeds: nothing 65,670 / 3.6%; strongroom 69,012 / 3.8%; reception rooms 59,550 / 4.8%); venture-heavy nothing (with reception rooms 92,815 against 97,332). |
