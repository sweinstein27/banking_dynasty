| 2026-11-06 | A2: the counting house replaces the three actions a turn. | Agreed plan, with the recommended answers to its three questions. **Capacity** (src/sim/capacity.ts, numbers in tuning.json `capacity`, which replaces `actions_per_turn`): each decision kind draws on one pool. Deals (1 per clerk): lend, buy_bond, invest_venture, call_loan, and any kind a later system adds unless listed. Reports (2 per correspondent): report. Opinions (2 per analyst): consult_analyst. The head's time (2, +2 with a partner): court_house, arrange_marriage, invest_family, borrow. Deals and reports fall back on the head's time when their pool is empty; opinions need an analyst. Free (no capacity): set_reserve, designate_heir, answer_letter, pay_slowly, suspend, hire, dismiss, build. With no living head the head's time is 0. `state.actions_this_turn` still counts decisions that used capacity (saves check it). **Staff** (a quarter): clerk 25 fl., correspondent 50, analyst 60, teller 20 (at most 5), partner 150 (at most 1, no desk). Each member has a name, a skill (drawn 2 to 6, +1 per 8 quarters served, at most 10) and one of four traits (careful, sanguine, sharp, muddled). The house starts with three fixed, named staff (Matthias Ebert and Konrad Haas, clerks; Simon Leclerc, correspondent), so a new game draws no extra numbers for them; three candidates per post are drawn at each End Turn (four rolls each). A hire starts work, and is paid, from next quarter; a dismissal works out the quarter, is paid for it, and leaves at End Turn (a hire of this quarter never starts). Every member but the partner needs a desk. **Facilities** (build, upkeep a quarter): counting room small (owned, 10, 6 desks), medium (2,000, 30, 12 desks), large (6,000, 80, 24 desks); strongroom (3,000, 25): panic withdrawals x0.75; archive (1,000, 10): payment records 24 quarters instead of 12 (for A1); courier stable (1,500, 30): warnings at once; reception rooms (2,000, 30): courting +10 opinion, marriages at 10 less opinion; warehouse (2,000, 20): 40 lots of storage (for A3). A facility is paid at once and works from next quarter; a larger room counts for hires made in the same quarter. **Wages and upkeep** are paid at End Turn after debts (ledger categories `wages`, one line per post, and `upkeep`, one line per room or facility; building is `building`). Wages may take cash below the reserve; if cash is short the house sells assets at a discount, and if that fails it is bankrupt. **Other staff:** each correspondent after the first takes 10% off report costs (at most 30%, for A1 to apply). Tellers: in a calm quarter each closes 2% more of the gap below the deposit level reputation draws (paid in the counting step, so before withdrawals; in a calm market no one withdraws, so the order changes nothing). A partner makes courting and the house's side of marriages 30% cheaper and courting +5 opinion. |

| 2026-11-06 | A2: analysts' opinions. | `consult_analyst` on a borrower asking for a loan this quarter writes "sound", "doubtful" or "unsound". Right with chance 0.55 + 0.04 x skill plus the trait (sharp +0.1, muddled -0.1), between 0.5 and 0.95; a wrong opinion is one of the other two, evenly. Decisions draw no random numbers, so each quarter's draws are rolled at the End Turn before (two per analyst working next quarter): an opinion cannot be re-rolled by asking again, and the most skilled analyst answers first. One opinion per borrower a quarter; opinions last the quarter. The truth comes from `bookVerdict`, which reads `Borrower.quality` (sound from 0.6, doubtful from 0.4) until A1's books replace it. |
| 2026-11-06 | A2: the advisor council. | The chief clerk (the longest-serving clerk), the head correspondent (the longest-serving correspondent) and the head's spouse each watch one topic: cash against a panic quarter's withdrawals; whether a panic warning is running (they sense it even without a courier stable, but sometimes wrongly); whether the heir would take over trained. Each End Turn every seat rolls once (in council order, filled or not): the advisor misreads with chance 0.35 - 0.03 x skill (at least 0.03; the spouse uses prudence), times the trait's tilt (careful: false alarms x1.5, missed trouble x0.5; sanguine the reverse; sharp both x0.5; muddled both x1.5; a spouse who is prudent, reckless or shrewd counts as careful, sanguine or sharp). They write before each Q1 (a routine letter) and otherwise only to warn, at most once every 4 quarters. Letters go to the news as kind "letter" with a title "From the chief clerk, Matthias Ebert" and text from data/counting.json. **Not the letter engine:** the D3 letters are events with choices to answer; advice has nothing to answer, so it is news. `advice.enabled: false` silences the council and its rolls (the test data uses it). |
