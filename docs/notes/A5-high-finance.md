| 2026-11-07 | A5: state loan contracts. | Each quarter each state with sizes in the data that has not defaulted, and has no loan open or placing, announces a loan with chance 4%, x2 from 1815 to 1824 (post-war loans), x0.3 from 1826 to 1829 (London stopped foreign loans after December 1825), x0.5 uneasy, none in a panic. Sizes: Prussia and Austria 300,000 to 600,000 fl., France 200,000 to 500,000, Bavaria 100,000 to 250,000, Naples 100,000 to 200,000 (steps of 50,000); coupon the state's plus 0 to 1% (steps of 0.25%); term 10, 15 or 20 years. The loan is open for bids the next quarter (`bid_contract`, one deal): a commission from 1% to 5% in steps of 0.5%; the gate is the state's bond gate (less the head's Connections) plus 10; the caution money (5% of the size) must fit in free cash. At End Turn the state takes the lowest score, commission less 0.02 x (reputation - 50) / 100, so ten points of reputation are worth 0.2% of commission; ties go to the better name, then to the player. Other houses bid when their wealth is at least half the size and their reputation clears the state's min_reputation plus 10: bold 80% of the time at 1.5% to 3.5%, cautious 50% at 2% to 4%. A winning house gains the commission on the whole size in wealth and 2 reputation. |
| 2026-11-07 | A5: placing a loan the house won. | The house pays the caution money at the award (a bid it can no longer cover lapses). The issue price is the state's market price at the award. Over the next two quarters investors take a share of what is left: 0.55 calm, 0.30 uneasy, 0.10 panic, x (0.6 + 0.8 x reputation / 100), x (1 - 0.5 x the state's risk), + 0.05 per correspondent, x a noise of up to 20% either way, at most 0.9. The house keeps its commission on each slice placed (`contract_fee`). At the close the caution money returns and the house buys the rest at the issue price less its commission (a firm contract); those bonds join `state.bonds`, so coupons, defaults, marking and forced sales already work, and they are redeemed at par when the term ends (`contract_redemption`). A carry the house cannot pay, even after forced sales, bankrupts it. If the state defaults during placing, nothing more is bought and the caution money is lost. Prestige: 75% or more placed +3 reputation (+5 for a loan of 400,000 fl. or more); under 40% "the issue stuck", -3. |
| 2026-11-07 | A5: lending to other houses. | In an uneasy or panicking market each house still trading, thinking -60 or better of the player and owing it nothing, may ask for credit (one roll): 0.3 x the larger of its distress (lost share of its starting wealth x 4, at most 1) and the mood's floor (uneasy 0.1, panic 0.4), x1.5 for a bold house; at most one request a quarter. It asks 5% of its wealth (at least 5,000 fl., to the 1,000) for 4 quarters, at up to 8% + 12% x urgency (uneasy 0.5, panic 1) + 4% if it dislikes the player. The player names the rate (`lend_house`, one deal), from 4% up to that limit. Gratitude: opinion +25 at 8% or less, +10 up to 12%, -5 above (gouging is remembered); repaying +5. Interest is paid each quarter from the house's wealth. While the loan runs the house's chance of failing in a panic is halved (`rescueFactor`, applied in `src/sim/houses.ts`). If it fails anyway its estate pays 30% of the claim (20% in a panic) at once. The refusal in the plan ("above its limit it refuses") became a check: like a merchant's request, the house names its limit, so the decision is refused instead of silently insulting it. |
| 2026-11-07 | A5: bankruptcy auctions. | A failed house puts up four lots from the data (its bonded warehouse 12% of its wealth, Rhine barges 8%, a parcel of shares in a listed company 8%, a broker's place 3%), skipped below 2,000 fl. or, for shares, before any company lists. A defaulted commercial loan or mortgage puts up the borrower's collateral at 50% of its value (barges for river freight and shipping, else a warehouse); bills put up nothing. Lots are open the next quarter (`bid_lot`, one deal, 30% to 100% of the appraisal). Each house with wealth at least 5x the appraisal bids half the time, at 75% calm, 60% uneasy, 45% panic, with up to 10% noise; the highest bid wins, a tie to the player; no bids means the lot is sold for scrap. The house pays its bid and counts the asset at its appraisal, so a bargain shows at once (`asset_revaluation`); shares join market holdings at the bourse's price. Warehouses earn 6% a year and burn with chance 0.5% a quarter; barges earn 12% x the mood's trade (1, 0.7, 0.4) and are lost with chance 1.5% a quarter, x2 in Q4 and Q1, x6 in a great storm; a broker's place earns 4% and gives +2 reputation (one at a time). `sell_asset` fetches 85% of the appraisal; a bank run sells assets whole at the venture discount (30%), then credit to houses at the loan discount. Owned assets are not insured. |
| 2026-11-07 | A5: insurance underwriting. | Two requests for cover a quarter, from the merchants the house knows: a ship's voyage (60%, 5,000 to 30,000 fl., one quarter) or a warehouse in Frankfurt, Hamburg, Mainz or Cologne (5,000 to 40,000 fl., four quarters). The premium is the expected loss over the term x 1.4: a ship's loss chance is 2% a quarter, x2 in winter (2.8% or 5.6% a voyage); a warehouse's 0.25% a quarter (1.4% a year). The house takes a line of 25%, 50% or 100% (`underwrite`, one deal) and the premium at once. A loss costs a ship's whole value less a salvage of 0% to 30%, or 30% to 100% of a warehouse. A great storm (4% a quarter, 8% in winter) makes every ship's loss x6 that quarter, so losses come together. The great fire of Hamburg (1842 Q2) burns each insured Hamburg warehouse with chance 0.5. Claims are paid at End Turn, with forced sales if cash is short; a house that still cannot pay is bankrupt. |
