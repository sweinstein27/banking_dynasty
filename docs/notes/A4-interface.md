| 2026-11-06 | A4: the grand-strategy interface. | Agreed plan, with the recommended answers to its four questions. The one-screen desk becomes a shell. **Top bar:** the house's crest, then seven readouts (cash, net worth with its trend over the last quarter, reputation, deposits, upkeep a quarter, the market, the date) and End the quarter with its seal. Each readout is a button; a click opens its breakdown. **Side menu:** icons with their names always shown, opening one panel at a time: the Gazette (home, and opened afresh each quarter), Ledger, Bourse, Houses, Family, Counting House, Letters (with a count while letters wait); the quill at the foot opens save, load and new game. **Panel slot** on the left, **the Desk** on the right (decision cards on a green blotter, the quarter's orders on a parchment sheet) and **For your attention** under it: alerts (letters, a panic warning, crisis measures open, debts falling due, no free cash, an old head with an untrained heir, actions left), each opening the panel that deals with it. At the end of a run the chronicle takes the Desk's place and the panels stay open, read-only, for the final books (this replaces "See the final books"). |
| 2026-11-06 | How are breakdowns built? | Pure functions in `src/ui/model/` (no React, no DOM), from the ledger and the sim's own functions only. Cash and deposits: last quarter's opening balance, each ledger category's flows, the closing balance, then this quarter's queued orders. Net worth: its parts (cash, loans, bonds, ventures, market holdings, less deposits and borrowings), then what changed it last quarter, rebuilt from that quarter's entries (assets count for it, deposits and borrowings against), then the last four year ends. The trend arrow compares the end of last quarter with its start. Before the first quarter ends, the opening books stand in for "last quarter". `tests/ui-breakdowns.test.ts` plays 20 quarters of the full economy and 20 of the panic seed and checks, before and after each quarter's orders, that every section adds up to its total, that the totals equal the readouts, and that net worth rebuilt from the ledger equals the sim's own figure for the quarter before. It does, so the ledger is a complete audit of net worth. |
| 2026-11-06 | What is "upkeep a quarter" before the counting house exists? | Interest credited to depositors plus interest on borrowings from other houses, at today's balances, split in the card. Deposit interest is owed, not paid in cash; the card says so. A2 adds wages and facilities to `upkeepLines` in `src/ui/model/breakdowns.ts`. |
| 2026-11-06 | What does the reputation card show? | The sim keeps no log of reputation changes, so the card shows what reputation does now (the deposits it draws, the withdrawals asked at today's mood, which states will sell bonds and from what reputation) and its history (this quarter's change from queued orders, and the last four year ends). **Follow-up for the integrator:** a "where it came from" list needs a reputation log in the sim (for example a `reputation_changes` list written wherever reputation moves). |
| 2026-11-06 | How do explanations work? | An underlined term is a button (`Term`, `src/ui/Term.tsx`) that opens a card on click, never on hover. A card's text can link to further cards with `[[id]]` or `[[id|words]]`; a link opens the next card in the same place, with Back and the trail shown. Card text lives in `src/ui/glossary.ts` as small functions of the game data, so numbers come from tuning. Terms keep their line height in prose; an invisible pseudo-element gives each a 44 px click area. `tests/ui-glossary.test.ts` checks that every link and every `<Term id>` in the UI has a card and that no card has an em dash. 36 cards. |
| 2026-11-06 | The look. | Dark lacquer chrome (#120d09 to #2c2117) with a gilt double rule (#b8943f) and corner flourishes drawn as inline SVG; parchment for every document (the Gazette, ledger pages, the bourse lists, letters, sheets, dialogs, the chronicle, breakdown and explanation cards). Light verdigris, amber and red for good, warning and bad on the dark chrome; the existing bottle green, amber and oxblood on paper. Fonts unchanged (IM Fell English, EB Garamond). Twenty-one line icons in `src/ui/icons.tsx`, drawn for the game in currentColor. No new dependencies: the breakdown and term cards use the Popover that comes with `radix-ui`. |
| 2026-11-06 | The Bourse, Counting House and Letters panels. | The Bourse has three ribbon tabs: merchants (this quarter's requests, then every borrower with a history or a loan, with the estimate and record), states (bond offers, why a state is closed, what you hold) and ventures (those backed, then those open). Each tab carries its decisions' buttons, which open the Desk's existing dialogs with nothing preselected, to keep merges with A1 and A3 small. The Counting House is a placeholder for A2: actions left, cash, reserve, free cash, Set reserves, and a note on staff and rooms. Letters lists this quarter's post (answered or waiting) with Read and answer, and every letter settled, paged. |
| 2026-11-06 | Keeping merges small. | Nothing in `src/sim/` changed. `DecisionDialogs.tsx` is untouched; `Desk.tsx` keeps its name and its coins code; `Letters.tsx` only exports its dialog. Panels reach the systems through `capacityLeft`, `describeSystemDecision` and the offer functions. The Houses view became a panel (house cards two to a page, standing on its own tab). |
